**Not in these corpora, by design (§11):** TIME_CLASS, query targets, the
lexical-holdout split (its hook is the reserved slice, refused for any
`train` split), any law other than the frozen one.

**Before the trainer consumes them (sub-project 2's first note):** an
autoregressive model is scored at every position, and a prefix of a
window is not a window of the law at its own endpoint (its start is
anchored to the sampled T). The pinned ceilings are for the full window;
prefix ceilings are computable by the same machinery but are not the
thirty artifacts. Which position the paper compares, and what the training
loss is a mixture over, is to be written down before the first
model-versus-ceiling number.
