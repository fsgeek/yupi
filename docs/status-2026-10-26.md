## Terms, for a reader arriving cold

- **Yupana:** the small stochastic OS kernel (4 threads, 2 CPUs, 2 locks, 1 device); its exact posteriors are the
  oracle.
- **Interfaces r0–r4:** how many fields of each event record the observer sees (r0 KIND only … r4 everything).
- **ε:** the scheduler's randomness (1 or ½).
- **k:** how many records of the window the observer has seen. "One step" predicts record k + 1; "two steps" the joint
  of records k + 1 and k + 2.
- **Pure / withheld:** a class whose next-KIND uncertainty is all randomness (G = 0), or that has at least 0.1 bits of
  uncertainty from hidden state (G ≥ 0.1). G is the mutual information between the next KIND and the hidden state,
  given the window.
- **μ (margin):** the TV distance from a pure class's exact law to the nearest withheld law.
- **B:** a learner's error bound, the 95 % quantile of TV(true law, output) on val.
- **Certifier:** prior-free; it certifies "pure" iff some pure law is within B of the output and every withheld law is
  farther than B.

## The story as it now stands (one world: Yupana, pattern observer, KIND)

**The question:** can an observer certify from a model's outputs that its uncertainty about the next event is
randomness and not hidden state? This is PACMI's testimony-vs-telemetry question in a world where the truth is known.

**One-sentence version (revised 10-26 after `certification-story-codex-review-2026-10-26.md`):** in a world where the
truth is known, the exact geometry (available before training) says how precise a learner must be for an auditor
without a prior to certify that its uncertainty is not substantial hidden state (≥ 0.1 bits). After calibration it
predicts how much will be certified. Two steps improved certification in 22 of 22 models and reached a majority of
pure windows in 15 of 22.

*Superseded wording, kept for the record:* "a guaranteed floor … can be computed before training … real learners
exceed the floor because their errors are not adversarial". The floor is a calibrated predictor, not a population
guarantee (only the pointwise lemma is a guarantee), and B needs a trained model. The non-adversarial-error account is
a hypothesis, not an identified cause.
