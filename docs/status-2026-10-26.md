## The story as it now stands (one world: Yupana, pattern observer, KIND)

**The question:** can an observer certify from a model's outputs that its uncertainty about the next event is
randomness and not hidden state? This is PACMI's testimony-vs-telemetry question in a world where the truth is known.

| claim | evidence | status |
|---|---|---|
| Exact margins: pure-randomness laws sit very close to near-pure laws; the nearest *substantial* hidden-state law is about 0.015 TV away at one step (k = 7) | `certification-margin-result-2026-10-22.md` | exact |
| Asking a second (third) question widens the margin, about 8× at k = 7; at k = 5, 0.04–0.11 → 0.16–0.22 → 0.24–0.37 | `two-step-margin-result-2026-10-23.md`, `three-step-margin-result-2026-10-26.md` | exact; D0–D3 hold |
| Average case: a prior and a second step are near-substitutes for a nearest-law monitor; their value grows as the learner worsens | `challenge-result-2026-10-25.md` (Codex's falsification design) | N1–N3 hold |
| **Worst case, without a prior** (the auditor's version): at a 95 % val error bound, one output certifies randomness only where the exact one-step margin is wide relative to learner error; two steps certify far more, in every cell | `certified-bound-result-2026-10-25.md`, `certified-bound-cells-result-2026-10-25.md` | B1–B4 hold; C2, C3 failed informatively |
| **A calibrated predictor of certifiability** built on a pointwise guarantee (μ > 2B and e ≤ B ⇒ certified), from the exact tables and val errors; within 0.03 of or below observed in 44 of 44 (3 tiny exceedances); median gaps 0.55 → 0.25 → 0.21 as size and class assumptions are dropped (descriptive, not causal) | `prospective-certification-*-result-*.md` (v1, v2, v3) | valid; tightness predictions partly failed |
| Learners do **not** err toward hidden state specifically (the PACMI-shaped "over-attribution" hypothesis) | `error-direction-result-2026-10-26.md` | W2, W3 failed |
| The observer's choices matter about as much as learner quality (R = 0.73, but R depends on the observer set: 0.12–2.74) | `learner-quality-result-2026-10-25.md`, `story-codex-review-2026-10-25.md` | scoped |

**One-sentence version (revised 10-26 after `certification-story-codex-review-2026-10-26.md`):** in a world where the
truth is known, the exact geometry (available before training) says how precise a learner must be for an auditor
without a prior to certify that its uncertainty is not substantial hidden state (≥ 0.1 bits). After calibration it
predicts how much will be certified. Two steps improved certification in 22 of 22 models and reached a majority of
pure windows in 15 of 22.

*Superseded wording, kept for the record:* "a guaranteed floor … can be computed before training … real learners
exceed the floor because their errors are not adversarial". The floor is a calibrated predictor, not a population
guarantee (only the pointwise lemma is a guarantee), and B needs a trained model. The non-adversarial-error account is
a hypothesis, not an identified cause.
